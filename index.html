About Jithin Sebastian

Jithin Sebastian is the Head of Engineering at Mozilor, the company behind the CookieYes consent management platform. He is also Mozilor's Information Security Officer and leads its R&D division. In 9 years of software engineering, more than 5 of them at Mozilor, he has grown from engineer to engineering leader. At CookieYes he scaled the platform from 100,000 to more than 1 million users, built data pipelines that handle over 3 billion requests a day, and architected its large-scale web scanning infrastructure. His work spans backend systems in Node.js, Go and Python, data infrastructure on Kafka and Elasticsearch, cloud platforms on AWS and GCP, and the security and privacy engineering that GDPR compliance depends on. As R&D lead, he drives the work of bringing AI into consent management.
